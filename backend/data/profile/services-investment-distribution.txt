Distribution Partner Sourcing Advisory
Find the Right Partners. Build a Stronger Presence.
The right distribution partner can shorten your time to market, reduce operational risks, and create long-term value. INPARTNER brings market insight and a structured approach to help you find partners that align with your business goals.
Partner Identification: Source and shortlist relevant distributors, resellers, and channel partners.
Due Diligence: Assess credibility, capabilities, and commercial alignment with your goals.
Negotiation Support: Support commercial terms, agreements, and partnership structure.
Ongoing Support: Monitor performance and provide guidance for a successful partnership.
Our approach, from search to strong partnerships: Assess (understand the market, opportunity, and potential risks), Strategize (define the right entry model and strategic priorities), Connect (identify relevant local partners and business opportunities), Execute (support the transition from market strategy to market presence).
Built for companies entering new markets, businesses expanding across Southeast Asia, foreign companies entering Indonesia, companies seeking local distribution partners, and investors evaluating new market opportunities.
